Cost of Capital Analysis — Multi-Company Assessment

A comprehensive analysis of the Cost of Capital for three prominent listed Indian companies — Solar Industries India Ltd, Deepak Nitrite Ltd, and Pidilite Industries Ltd for FY2024-25.

TypeAcademic Project
InstitutionGreat Lakes Institute of Management, Gurgaon
FocusWACC, CAPM, DGM
RoleGroup Member (Group 8)
Objective

Analyze the Cost of Capital for three distinct companies across different sectors using advanced financial modeling techniques to derive their Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC).

Methodology & Process

The study applied multiple valuation models to compute the cost of equity and debt, followed by determining the overall cost of capital using market-value weights.

01

Cost of Equity Calculation

Applied both the Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM) and Dividend Growth Model (DGM) to determine the cost of equity for each firm.

02

Cost of Debt Assessment

Derived the after-tax cost of debt from the published financials of Solar Industries, Deepak Nitrite, and Pidilite Industries.

03

WACC Computation

Computed the Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC) using market-value weights for a precise reflection of each company's capital structure.

Key Findings (FY 2024-25)
  • Solar Industries: Blended Cost of Equity (Ke) of 12.81%, with a capital structure predominantly weighted towards equity (99.52%).
  • Deepak Nitrite: Blended Ke of 11.83%, with a debt weight of 4.96% and an after-tax Cost of Debt (Kd) of 3.66%.
  • Pidilite Industries: Blended Ke of 11.92%, with the highest after-tax Kd among the three at 9.32%, though equity still makes up 99.65% of the capital structure.
