Hindustan Unilever — Ratio Analysis

A group written assignment analyzing Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL) using financial ratios to formulate a data-backed investment decision.

TypeAcademic Assignment
InstitutionGreat Lakes Institute of Management, Gurgaon
CompanyHindustan Unilever Limited
RoleGroup Member (Group 3)
Objective

Analyze Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL), an FMCG market leader, using financial ratios to make an informed investment decision for the Financial Accounting and Decision Making (FADM) course.

Why HUL?

We chose HUL for its universal presence across Indian households, its legacy of over 90 years, and its diverse portfolio spanning home care, beauty, personal care, and foods & refreshment.

01

Everyday Presence

HUL brands like Dove, Surf Excel, and Kissan are interwoven into the fabric of daily routines across the country.

02

Diverse Portfolio

With 50+ brands, HUL's risk is distributed across multiple essential consumer categories, providing a balanced financial picture.

03

Innovation & Adaptability

HUL continues to evolve with consumer needs, showing strategic adaptability that impacts its financial performance positively.

Methodology & Analysis
  • Vertical Analysis: Examined the income statement to track revenue growth and COGS trends (e.g., COGS fluctuating between 46–52% of revenue, peaking in Mar-23).
  • Ratio Analysis: Evaluated the company's strengths and weaknesses through a comprehensive set of financial ratios to form a robust investment view.

Key Takeaways
  • Demonstrated how a company's market dominance and strategic decisions translate directly into its financial metrics.
  • Highlighted the importance of contextualizing financial ratios within the broader FMCG market landscape.
