Quick Clean Laundry Solutions — Finance Internship

A dual-project Summer Internship: a three-stream financial performance evaluation of Quick Clean's on-premise laundry network, and a portfolio-wide brand intelligence dashboard for its Marriott India account.

RoleFinance Intern
Duration1 Apr – 15 Jun 2026
Objective

Support the Founder's Office at Quick Clean — an on-premise laundry (OPL) and linen-management company serving hospitality and healthcare clients — across two mandates: evaluating site-level financial and capex performance across the OPL network (Project 1), and sizing and visualizing the white-space opportunity within its flagship Marriott India account (Project 2).

Methodology & Process

Project 1 ran through five phases, from site immersion to a network-wide profitability review; Project 2 applied the same underlying discipline — baseline construction, like-for-like cost mapping, cost-driver decomposition — to a single flagship account.

01

Orientation & Site Immersion

Began with hands-on site visits to the Westin Gurgaon OPL installation, operating washing machines, presses and geysers alongside the site team — grounding the later financial analysis in the physical cost drivers behind cost-per-kg and utilisation metrics.

02

Three-Stream Data Collection

Collected data from Sales SPOC (deal closure, objections, contract terms), Operations (machine logs, turnaround time, staffing, maintenance) and Finance (site-level P&L, capex records, cost-per-kg, breakeven volume) to build a 360-degree view of each site.

03

Brownfield / Greenfield Dashboarding

Built an Excel-based comparative dashboard distinguishing Brownfield sites (replacing an existing setup) from Greenfield sites (no prior laundry infrastructure), since the two differ systematically in ramp-up curve and sales objections.

04

Structured Case Study Programme

Authored ten detailed site case studies — deal closure, objections, stakeholders, pre-implementation baseline, post-implementation results, P&L trajectory — following a template approved directly by the CEO.

05

Knowledge Centre & Decision-Support Tools

Designed, built and deployed the Quick Clean Knowledge Centre (Flask/Jinja2, GitHub, Streamlit) to make the case studies searchable, plus a CO₂ emissions calculator and a brand/strategic intelligence report generator.

06

Pan-India Profitability Review

Extended the validated site-level P&L template across the full OPL network to rank sites by margin and flag underperforming sites for management action.

07

Marriott Portfolio Deep-Dive (Project 2)

Compiled the full 282-property Marriott India portfolio, mapped post-partnership invoicing against pre-partnership baselines property-by-property, and decomposed savings into manpower, utility, chemical, capex-avoidance and space-monetisation drivers.

08

Brand Intelligence Dashboard

Built an interactive, filterable dashboard (HTML/CSS/JavaScript, deployed on Vercel) covering portfolio overview, savings analysis, sustainability impact and a phased rollout timeline, then presented the full walkthrough to the CEO.

Assumptions & Scope
  • The detailed financial walk-through is built around one representative case study, selected for capturing a complete commercial-to-operational arc — its specific monthly figures should not be read as the network-wide average, though it was validated qualitatively against the Pan-India Profitability Review.
  • Both projects were compressed into a roughly ten-week window; site performance was captured at a fixed point (typically Month 6 post-commissioning) rather than tracked longitudinally over a full contract lifecycle.
  • All financial and operational figures originate from Quick Clean's internal Sales, Operations and Finance records (including MIS data reconciled during the internship) — the underlying data was not independently audited.
  • No published India-specific OPL cost-per-kg or utilisation benchmarks exist; literature-derived benchmarks from European and North American facility-management studies were applied as a reasonable approximation rather than a locally calibrated standard.
  • The Knowledge Centre is deliberately internal-access-only given deal-data sensitivity, so its usage impact could not be independently measured within the internship timeframe.
Key Findings

Representative site, before vs. after Quick Clean (Month 6) — turnaround time, linen damage rate, cost per kg and required par stock all improved materially.

Machine utilisation at the representative site rose from 42% (Month 1) to 85% (Month 6), moving the site from an EBITDA loss to a 27.5% EBITDA margin.

  • Deal cycles averaged three to six months from first meeting to commissioning, with vendor lock-in the most common client objection — addressed through total-cost-of-ownership comparisons and reference-site visits.
  • Fixed costs (depreciation, maintenance contracts, supervisor salaries) accounted for 60–70% of total site costs; cost per kg ranged from ₹9 to ₹19 across sites, driven primarily by machine utilisation.
  • Of 282 Marriott India properties mapped, 265 remain outside Quick Clean's current scope — a substantial, already-warm expansion opportunity within a single enterprise relationship.
  • 38 structured site case studies were developed in total, integrating Sales, Operations and Finance data into a single reusable institutional format.
  • Three supplementary decision-support tools were built beyond the core Knowledge Centre: a site-level CO₂ emissions calculator, an internal AI knowledge assistant, and a brand/strategic intelligence report generator.
Key Takeaways
  • In a capital-intensive, contract-based service business, financial performance, operational metrics and commercial strategy cannot be analysed in isolation — they must be triangulated together to produce genuinely actionable insight.
  • The consistent throughline in both projects was converting fragmented, person-dependent, spreadsheet-bound data into structured, reusable digital assets that Quick Clean's teams can keep using after the internship ends.
  • Objection handling is a competitive moat: the same objections recur almost identically across the sales pipeline, so a documented, data-backed response playbook compounds in value with every new deal.
  • Prioritising the Marriott white-space opportunity (265 of 282 properties) using the phased, aging-prioritised rollout already built into the dashboard was flagged as a high-impact, medium-effort recommendation to Quick Clean's leadership.
